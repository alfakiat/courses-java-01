Concept 3.1

Computer Languages

Programming languages are categorized into three main levels based on how close they are to the hardware versus how close they are to human language.

Instructor Note

Cycle through the language levels to show how programming evolved from hardware-dependent binary codes to abstract, human-readable instructions.
